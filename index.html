About Jenny

Jenny Slyder is an Arizona acoustic performer known for warm vocals, familiar songs, and an easygoing stage presence. Her sets blend country, classic rock, Americana, folk, and singer-songwriter favorites.

Jenny is available for restaurants, local venues, private events, community gatherings, and relaxed acoustic nights throughout Arizona.
